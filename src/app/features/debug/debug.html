About Our Madrasa

An Islamiyya Built Around Knowledge and Tarbiyah

Al-Fiqh Madrasa provides structured Islamic education designed to help students learn, practise and live by their faith.

Our approach brings together Qur'anic learning, Tajweed, Arabic language and broader Islamic studies while encouraging good character, responsibility and a strong connection with the Muslim community.

The programme is intended to support children, young people and adults at different stages of their Islamic learning journey.

Our Educational Focus

Knowledge that shapes character

We aim to make Islamic learning meaningful beyond the classroom—helping students understand their religion and apply its teachings in everyday life.
